Auth0 setup required

Sign-in isn’t active yet. Add your Auth0 domain and clientId in auth.js, then set the callback URLs in your Auth0 application.

  • Callback: http://localhost:8000/login.html
  • Logout: http://localhost:8000/index.html
  • Web Origin: http://localhost:8000

Your scans, streak and badges live in this browser, signed in or not. There is no copy on a server and no sync between devices. Clear your browser data and the history is gone with it.

Signing in only decides which of those records are yours on this machine. The scanner never asks for an account, and no copy of your photo is kept. There is a longer note on what an account does if you want to read it first.
