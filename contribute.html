Add a flower to the training set

Nothing leaves this page on its own. Your photos are measured here, in your browser, against the same size, sharpness and exposure gates the collection runs. What passes is cropped to the 448 pixel square the model reads and packed into one file, which you send by mail or attach to an issue.

There is no upload endpoint behind this, and saying otherwise would be a convenient lie. What the set holds today and how it is measured is written up in the dataset page.

What are you adding?

Your photos

One flower filling most of the frame, in daylight, in focus. Up to eight files at a time, 10 MB each, as JPEG, PNG or WebP.

    The name

    These are the 107 names Flora-Micro reads inside your own tab, and they are the only model vocabulary the site publishes. If the flower you photographed is not in the list, the second path above is the right one.

    A name the model has never seen needs a folder of its own before it can be learned, and the collection is aiming at 300 photos for every species. One good set is a start on that, not a finished class.

    An unchecked name is worth much less to a training set than a name with a source behind it, so this one is not optional.

    Credit and licence

    Every photo in the set carries the licence its photographer chose and the name they are credited under, recorded one record per image rather than summarised. Yours would be recorded the same way, so this is the part that decides who may use your photograph and how.

    A name, a handle, anything you answer to. Under CC0 you can leave this blank.

    When and where

    Both are optional and both are useful: a date and a place turn a photograph into an observation, which is what makes a bloom season or a range worth trusting later.

    Read from the file when it says so, and yours to change.

    Where you found it

    Nothing is asked for until you press that. The coordinates go into the file you send and nowhere else.
