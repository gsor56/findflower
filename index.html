How it works

Three unhurried steps, from a photograph to real understanding.

  1. Step 01

    Capture or upload

    Point your camera at a plant, or drop in a photo you already have. One clear frame of a bloom or a leaf is enough to begin. There is nothing to install and no account to make first.

  2. Step 02

    The model reads it

    Your photo is sent to a Vision Transformer running under ONNX Runtime, which returns a species and a confidence score. The image is used for that prediction and is not stored afterwards. The privacy page sets out what happens to it.

  3. Step 03

    Learn the species

    A name on its own teaches you nothing. Wikidata supplies the binomial and family, Trefle the growth habit and light needs, Wikipedia the plate and the article. Those four requests assemble into the record shown here.

A quiet tool for
a complex world.

FindFlower exists to close a small, ordinary gap: you can see a plant perfectly clearly and still have no way to name it. Give it one photograph and it returns a species, a confidence score, and the botanical record standing behind them.

Software should enhance our connection to the physical world, not abstract it. The identification engine is designed to be unobtrusive, highly responsive, and strictly focused on delivering precise information when you need it.

FindFlower is built slowly and deliberately, with every detail considered from the moment you upload an image to the clarity of the result you read. What reaches you should always feel calm, considered, and genuinely useful.

Why FindFlower

Built out of things you can check.

Vision Transformer pipeline

The classifier is a Vision Transformer exported to ONNX and served through ONNX Runtime, across 116 botanical classes. Every prediction comes back with its confidence and its closest alternatives, not a single unexplained answer.

Live Wikidata & Trefle encyclopedia

A name is where the work starts. Wikidata contributes the structured taxonomy, Trefle the growing data, Wikipedia the plate and the article. Each is fetched when you ask for it, not frozen into a copy that quietly ages.

Zero registration required

No account, no API key, no install. Open the scanner and identify. The only record kept is the scan history your own browser holds, and you can clear it whenever you like.
