Rules

  1. Say where you found it. A photo with no place and no date is hard to help with.
  2. Corrections are the point. If the model named it wrongly, post the right name and how you know.
  3. One question per post. Two plants in one thread means one of them gets no answer.
  4. No selling, no promotion.
  5. Do not post a location you would not want visited. Rare plants get dug up.

Where this lives

Post text is stored on the FindFlower server, so it reaches other people and other devices. Photos are not sent: your identifications and their thumbnails stay in this browser.

The feed reads twenty posts at a time and keeps only the rows near your scroll position in the page, so a long history does not slow the phone down.

Field logs are the long-form write-ups. Posts here are short.
